Know when to seek urgent help

Chest pain, severe breathing trouble, fainting, stroke symptoms, severe bleeding, confusion, blue lips, or suicidal thoughts need immediate emergency care.

Important Note

Screening is not diagnosis

These indices organize entered risk factors for educational use. A qualified medical professional must interpret symptoms, measurements, laboratory values, and appropriate tests.
